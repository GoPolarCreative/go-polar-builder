## 2. Register the webhook

**Settings → Notifications → Webhooks → Create webhook.**

- **Event:** `Order payment` (`orders/paid`)
- **Format:** JSON
- **URL:** `https://build.itscold.com.au/api/webhooks/shopify`
- **API version:** 2025-01 or later

Shopify shows a **signing secret** once created. Copy it into Vercel as `SHOPIFY_WEBHOOK_SECRET`.

Every request is HMAC-verified against the raw body. Without the secret the endpoint refuses every
webhook rather than trusting it: accepting unverified payment webhooks would let anyone create paid
jobs by posting JSON at it.

**If you skip it:** customers pay and nothing happens. No job, no build link, no email. They have
been charged $220 and have no website and no way in.

## 3. Copy the ids into Vercel

```
SHOPIFY_STORE_DOMAIN=itscold.myshopify.com
SHOPIFY_WEBHOOK_SECRET=

# Required. Shopify refuses a subscription line without its selling plan id.
SHOPIFY_VARIANT_WEBSITE_HOSTING_AUSTRALIA=
SHOPIFY_SELLING_PLAN_WEBSITE_HOSTING_AUSTRALIA=
SHOPIFY_VARIANT_DOMAIN_1_YEAR=
SHOPIFY_SELLING_PLAN_DOMAIN_1_YEAR=
SHOPIFY_VARIANT_EMAIL_HOSTING=
SHOPIFY_SELLING_PLAN_EMAIL_HOSTING=
```

Variant id: Products → the product → Variants → the number at the end of the URL.
Selling plan id: Appstle → the plan → its numeric id.

**The three new products need nothing here.** Their variant ids were read off the store when they
were created and are recorded in `shared/pricing.ts`, so they work as soon as they are published.
Setting `SHOPIFY_VARIANT_BUILD_TOKEN` and friends overrides the recorded value, which is how you
would point the app at a different store.

**Also set both of these.**

```
SHOPIFY_ADMIN_API_TOKEN=
```

Custom app token, scopes `read_orders` and `read_products`. Three jobs: the nightly sweep that finds
paid orders whose webhook never arrived, the billing policy check, and the draft check. Without it
the app reports "cannot verify" rather than passing.

```
SHOPIFY_STOREFRONT_TOKEN=
```

Scope `unauthenticated_write_checkouts`. A cart permalink carries only **one** selling plan, and a
customer taking hosting **and** email is two subscriptions. Without this the app refuses that
checkout and says why rather than quietly dropping a line. Treat it as required.

## What the app checks for itself

Before building any checkout link it reads the store through the Admin API and **refuses** if:

- the product is **not active** — a draft or archived product cannot be bought, and the error says
  so by name. Publishing takes effect immediately, with no code change and nothing to remember.
- a monthly product **does not bill every 1 MONTH**. The domain product spent a period with a plan
  *named* "Monthly Subscription" whose policy was interval YEAR. Shopify does not object to that: the
  name is a label, the policy is the behaviour. The app would have advertised $5.50 a month while the
  store charged $5.50 a year, and nobody finds out for twelve months.

`GET /api/health` shows the same report. With `ENABLE_LIVE_PAYMENTS=1` the API refuses to boot while
anything is outstanding, and names every item.
